### Executive Summary

As you prepare to kick off your enterprise modernization, this project marks a pivotal turning point in Hitachi High-Tech Corporation’s illustrious history. For three decades, your legacy SAP ERP backbone supported remarkable global growth; however, an accumulation of approximately 9,000 custom add-ons has created architectural calcification, hindering operational agility across your 26 operating countries. By undertaking this greenfield transformation—anchored by **SAP S/4HANA Cloud (Two-Tier Edition)**, **SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP)**, and **SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)**—you are strategically positioning your enterprise to dominate the evolving high-tech semiconductor and medical diagnostics industries.

Our engagement strategy focuses on three transformative business outcomes:
1. **Slashing Total Cost of Ownership (TCO):** Reducing your custom-code footprint by an extraordinary 94% (from ~9,000 add-ons to fewer than 800 standard/BTP-hosted extensions) by adopting a strict **Fit-to-Standard** delivery ethos.
2. **Accelerating Global Agility:** Compressing new subsidiary onboarding cycles and localized market rollouts from 12 months to under 8 weeks through standard APIs and S/4HANA Public Cloud subsidiary deployments.
3. **Establishing Real-Time Operational Intelligence:** Powering instantaneous global decision-making through SAC live-data connectivity across global manufacturing lines, supply chain hubs, and field service networks.

During the upcoming Discovery and Explore phases, our engagement team will employ two complementary analysis frameworks: **PESTLE Analysis** to navigate geopolitical semiconductor mandates and regional healthcare regulations (FDA/IVDR), and **Design Thinking** to empathize with your business users, rapidly co-design intuitive SAP Fiori workflows, and overcome change resistance.

Our collaborative project team pairs specialized SAP consulting capabilities (Functional Leadership, BTP Architecture, SAC Data Modeling, OCM, and Activate Project Management) with your dedicated Business Process Owners (BPOs). Supported by a formal Clean Core Governance Board, we ensure your investments yield rapid time-to-value while preserving architectural integrity. 

We welcome you to this transformational partnership and look forward to executing a successful Prepare phase kickoff.
